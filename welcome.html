Welcome to Flash Video Downloader

Three steps to your first download. Takes under a minute.

1

Open a page with a playable video

Start playback on the site. The extension listens to network traffic on the active tab.

2

Click the extension icon

Detected MP4, WEBM and M3U8 streams appear in the popup. Preview before you save.

3

Download

Pause, resume or cancel anytime. History stays on your device — no cloud, no tracking.

Does not work on DRM platforms such as YouTube, Netflix, Disney+ or Twitch. Only openly accessible streams you have the right to save.
Free tier: 1 download every 2 hours. Optional Pro unlocks unlimited downloads via Ko-fi (Settings → Pro). Core detection works without Pro.
