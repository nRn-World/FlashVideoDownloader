Privacy Policy – Flash Video Downloader

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Developer: nRn World (bynrnworld@gmail.com)

Who is responsible

The data controller for anything described in this policy is nRn World, reachable at bynrnworld@gmail.com. We are established in Sweden and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies to us. There is no data protection officer, because we do not carry out processing of the kind that requires one.

The short version of our position: the extension is built so that almost nothing leaves your device. We do not have user accounts, we do not run analytics, and the only optional server contact is a Free-tier rate-limit check that is not enabled in this build — see the dedicated section below.

Summary

Download history, settings and your optional Pro license key stay on your device. We do not collect video URLs or browsing history on a server.

To enforce the Free limit of 1 download every 2 hours after reinstall, the extension may contact a small rate-limit service. That service does not receive page URLs or file names. It stores a one-way hash of your network address and a timestamp for up to 2 hours, then deletes it.

Ads

The Free tier shows small promotional banners ("house ads") inside the extension popup — for example an invitation to upgrade to Pro, support the developer, or rate the extension. These banners are bundled with the extension: they contain no third-party code, call no ad network, and do not track you. Clicking a banner simply opens the linked page in a new tab.

Some banners are affiliate links for third-party products. If you click one and make a purchase, the developer may earn a commission. Clicking simply opens the partner's website in a new tab; that site then processes your data under its own privacy policy. The extension itself sends no data to the partner — clicks are only counted locally on your device.

Pro users see no ads. Activating a Pro license removes every banner.

Data stored on your device

  • Download history – filename, sanitized URL (without query tokens), file size, timestamp. Stored in Chrome local storage.
  • Settings – language preference and auto-delete history option.
  • Active downloads – progress state during the browser session (session storage).
  • Pro license (optional) – if you purchase a Pro upgrade via Ko-fi, a hashed license token is stored locally in Chrome storage. The plaintext key is not kept in the extension source. No payment information is collected by the extension.

Data processed temporarily

  • Media URLs detected from network headers and page elements on tabs you interact with while using the extension.
  • Video files downloaded at your request are saved directly to your computer via the browser download mechanism.

What we do NOT do

  • No analytics or tracking.
  • No third-party ad networks, ad identifiers, or ad tracking — Free-tier banners are bundled with the extension and load no external code.
  • No data is shared with affiliate partners; clicking a partner link is counted only on your device.
  • No user accounts.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data with third parties.
  • No cloud upload of your videos or browsing history.
  • No bypass of DRM-protected or restricted streaming platforms.
  • No collection of payment information (optional Pro purchases are completed on Ko-fi; Ko-fi/PayPal process payment under their own policies).
  • No storage of raw IP addresses — the optional rate-limit service stores only a one-way hash for up to 2 hours.

Free-tier rate limit (not active in this build)

This build of the extension makes no request to any rate-limit service. The endpoint address is empty in the shipped code, so the code path is never taken: no network identifier, no hash and no timestamp leaves your device. The Free limit of 1 download every 2 hours is enforced entirely locally.

The section below describes what would be processed if we ever enable the optional service in a future version. It is written now so that the description never has to be written after the fact.

  • Why it would exist: browsers delete an extension’s local storage when it is uninstalled, which would otherwise let someone reset the Free limit by reinstalling.
  • What would be processed: a one-way cryptographic hash of a coarse network identifier, plus the time of the request. No video URL, no filename, no browsing history, no licence key, and never the raw network address itself.
  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in preventing trivial abuse of the Free tier. We have kept the data to the minimum needed for that purpose and it cannot be used to identify you in practice.
  • Retention: at most 2 hours, after which it is deleted. It is not used for profiling, marketing or any other purpose, and it is never sold or shared.
  • Transfers: if the service is hosted outside your country, we would rely on the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses and would not enable it without them in place.
  • Pro users are never rate-limited and would never trigger this check.

Enabling it would be a change to this policy, and the “Last updated” date above would change with it.

Pro upgrade (optional paid feature)

Flash Video Downloader is free to install. The Free tier allows 1 video download every 2 hours and 1 concurrent download. An optional Pro upgrade (EUR 10.99, one-time lifetime license) unlocks unlimited downloads, 3 concurrent downloads, and unlimited local history. Payment is processed on Ko-fi (https://ko-fi.com/s/72a48b875e). After payment, Ko-fi shows your license key. You activate it in Settings → Pro. The extension stores a hashed license token locally, not the key in source code. No payment card data is collected or processed by the extension.

Permissions explained

  • webRequest / host access – Read response headers (Content-Type, size) to identify video files on pages you visit.
  • activeTab / scripting – Scan the current page for video elements only when you open the extension popup.
  • storage – Save local preferences and download history.
  • downloads / offscreen – Save merged video files to your device.

Data retention

Download history can be auto-deleted after 24 hours (enabled by default). You can clear history anytime in Settings.

Your rights

Because we hold almost no personal data about you, most requests resolve themselves: your download history and settings can be viewed and deleted by you at any time under Settings, and uninstalling the extension deletes everything we ever stored. Beyond that, under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you, and receive a copy (Art. 15);
  • have inaccurate data rectified (Art. 16);
  • have your data erased (Art. 17);
  • have processing restricted (Art. 18);
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21) — the only such processing we ever carry out is the rate-limit check described above, and objecting to it means we may have to treat you as rate-limited locally instead;
  • data portability (Art. 20), which in practice means your local history export;
  • not be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing (Art. 22) — we run no such decisions.

Write to bynrnworld@gmail.com and we will answer within one month. If you are not satisfied, you have the right to complain to the Swedish data protection authority, Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY), or to the supervisory authority in your own country. You can always contact your local authority without going through us first.
